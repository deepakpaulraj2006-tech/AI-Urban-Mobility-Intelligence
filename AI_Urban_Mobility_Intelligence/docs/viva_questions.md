# Viva Questions
1. What is EDA? — Exploratory Data Analysis.
2. Why Pandas? — Tabular cleaning and analysis.
3. Why Plotly? — Interactive charts.
4. Why Streamlit? — Fast Python dashboard development.
5. What is classification? — Predicting a categorical target.
6. What is the target? — congestion_level.
7. Why train/test split? — To estimate unseen-data performance.
8. What is stratification? — Preserving class proportions.
9. What is data leakage? — Using information unavailable at prediction time.
10. What is precision? — Correctness among predicted positives.
11. What is recall? — Coverage of actual positives.
12. What is F1? — Harmonic mean of precision and recall.
13. What is a confusion matrix? — Actual vs predicted class counts.
14. Why Random Forest? — Nonlinear ensemble model.
15. Why Logistic Regression? — Interpretable baseline.
16. Why Decision Tree? — Rule-based nonlinear model.
17. What is feature engineering? — Creating useful variables.
18. Why one-hot encoding? — Represent categories numerically.
19. Why scaling? — Helps scale-sensitive models.
20. What is a hotspot? — Location with elevated traffic-risk indicators.
21. Is the hotspot score official? — No.
22. Is the data real? — No, it is simulated.
23. Why synthetic data? — Reproducibility and self-contained demonstration.
24. What-if simulation? — Compare model outputs under changed inputs.
25. Future scope? — Real GPS/sensor feeds and validated models.
